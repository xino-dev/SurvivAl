// ASK THE AI

TYPE IT IN YOUR OWN WORDS

No commands to memorize. Everything runs on your device — your questions never leave your phone.

YOUHow do I find and purify water in the wild?
SURVIVALLook for moving water first — streams beat stagnant pools. Boil for one minute, use purification tablets, or build a simple sand-and-charcoal filter. Dew off leaves at dawn is a quiet backup.
YOUI am lost — how do I navigate without a compass?
SURVIVALStick to a downhill line: water flows toward people. Use the sun's arc for east–west, mark your trail, and keep a pace count so you know how far you've moved.
