# SurvivAl — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-09-09**

SurvivAl ("the app") is an offline survival toolkit developed by xino-dev.

## Short version

The app works **entirely on your device**. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your
personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

## What the app stores on your device

The following data is saved **only on your phone** (in the app's private storage) and never leaves it:

- **Conversations** with the built-in AI advisor
- **Waypoints** you save on the GPS map (names, coordinates, icons)
- **Settings** (language, engine options, checklist state)
- **AI model files** you choose to download or import

## Network access

The app requests internet access for exactly one purpose: **downloading AI model files** you choose
to download (from public model hosts such as huggingface.co). These downloads contain no personal
data. If you never download a model, the app does not use the network at all.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Location (fine/coarse) | Shows your position, heading and waypoint bearings on the GPS radar. Used in the foreground only. |
| Notifications | Shows a progress notification while a model downloads or the AI is working. |
| Internet | Model downloads only (see above). |

## AI answers

SurvivAl's AI runs fully on your device from a model file you install. Answers are generated offline
and are never sent to any server. The AI can make mistakes — always verify anything important. The
app is not a professional rescue or medical service; in a real emergency contact your local
emergency number.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same URL with an updated date.
