PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 9, 2026
Short version: SurvivAl works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit or share any of your personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

WHO WE ARE

SurvivAl ("the app") is an offline survival toolkit developed by Xinodev.

WHAT THE APP STORES ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is saved only on your phone, in the app's private storage, and never leaves it:

  • Conversations with the built-in AI advisor
  • Waypoints you save on the GPS map (names, coordinates, icons)
  • Settings (language, engine options, checklist state)
  • AI model files you choose to download or import

You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

NETWORK ACCESS

The app requests internet access for exactly one purpose: downloading AI model files that you choose to download from public model hosts such as huggingface.co. These downloads contain no personal data. If you never download a model, the app does not use the network at all.

AI ANSWERS

SurvivAl's AI runs fully on your device from a model file you install. Answers are generated offline and are never sent to any server. The AI can make mistakes — always verify anything important. The app is not a professional rescue or medical service; in a real emergency, contact your local emergency number.

CHILDREN

The app is not directed at children under 13.
